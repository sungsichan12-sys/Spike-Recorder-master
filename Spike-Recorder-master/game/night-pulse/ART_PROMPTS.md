# Night Pulse artwork

The two shipped PNG assets were created with the built-in imagegen tool.
`rooftop.png` uses this prompt:

> Create one ORIGINAL hand-painted 16:9 background illustration for a polished desktop rhythm game called Night Pulse. Dark futuristic rooftop concert above a Korean coastal city at night, deep midnight blue and black palette, luminous cyan and violet aurora ribbons sweeping across the upper sky, distant city lights and subtle atmospheric haze. The central 55 percent should stay compositionally quiet and low contrast so bright falling music notes and UI can be drawn clearly on top; reserve the right side for a separate character overlay. Rich layered environment, crisp art direction, high quality game key art, no text, no logos, no interface elements, no musical notes, no character. Full-bleed rectangular artwork.

`drummer.png` uses this generation prompt:

> Create a single ORIGINAL human character illustration for a desktop rhythm game, with a truly hand-drawn 2D game art feel. A Korean young adult street drummer, full body, standing naturally with two drumsticks, short dark hair, focused but warm expression, practical navy bomber jacket over charcoal shirt, loose black trousers and sneakers. Subtle cyan and violet rim light ties the figure to a nighttime rooftop concert, but clothing is grounded and understated. Art direction: refined indie-game concept painting with visible pencil/brush texture, confident anatomy, believable hands, clean silhouette readable at 300-pixel height, restrained colors, deliberately imperfect human drawing details. Avoid glossy airbrushed surfaces, hyper-detailed fantasy styling, anime eyes, AI-looking symmetry, excessive accessories, and photorealism. Isolated full figure with generous transparent margins. No background, no text, no logo, no UI.

The final character was edited from that generation with this prompt:

> Edit this exact character illustration. Keep the human drummer, pose, hand-drawn painterly look, face, clothes, drumsticks, proportions, and transparent background. Remove every recognizable brand logo or swoosh from both shoes and replace with plain unbranded sneaker panels. Also slightly simplify the airbrushed halo around the silhouette so the result reads as a deliberate indie game illustration at small size. Do not add any text or scenery. Preserve transparency.
